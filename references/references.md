# Verified Research References

This file contains a curated collection of scholarly references related to detecting citation drift in AI-generated literature summaries, citation hallucination, citation verification, factual consistency, scientific summarization, and evidence-grounded generation.

**Verification principle:** Each entry was checked against a scholarly publisher, conference archive, DOI record, PubMed, or an official research repository. Links point to scholarly or official project pages where possible.

## Survey and Review / Citation Integrity

### 1. Citation Drift: Measuring Reference Stability in Multi-Turn LLM Conversations
**Author:** Gokul Srinath Seetha Ram  
**Year:** 2025  
**Venue:** Proceedings of the Third Workshop for Artificial Intelligence for Scientific Publications (WASP)  
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2025.wasp-main.20/)  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2025.wasp-main.20  
**Relevance:** Introduces citation drift and metrics including citation stability, fabrication rate, drift rate, drift entropy, and willingness-to-cite. 

### 2. Fabrication and Errors in the Bibliographic Citations Generated by ChatGPT
**Authors:** William H. Walters, Esther Isabelle Wilder  
**Year:** 2023  
**Venue:** Scientific Reports, 13, Article 14045  
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5)  
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5  
**Relevance:** Systematically evaluates fabricated and erroneous citations generated by ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-4.

### 3. Evaluating the Integrity of LLM-Generated Citations: Prevalence and Risks of Fabricated References in Scientific Literature
**Authors:** Pablo Picazo-Sanchez, Lara Ortiz-Martin  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Data, 11(5), 122  
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/data11050122)  
**DOI:** 10.3390/data11050122  
**Relevance:** Studies fabricated scientific references generated by nine LLMs and examines fabricated bibliographic data in published literature.

### 4. Hallucinated Citations Produced by Generative Artificial Intelligence May Constitute Research Misconduct When Citations Function as Data in Scholarly Papers
**Authors:** David B. Resnik, Mohammad Hosseinib  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Accountability in Research  
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/08989621.2026.2645390)  
**DOI:** 10.1080/08989621.2026.2645390  
**Relevance:** Examines the research-integrity implications of fabricated AI-generated citations.

### 5. LLM Hallucinations in the Wild: Large-Scale Evidence from Non-Existent Citations
**Authors:** Zhenyue Zhao, Yihe Wang, Toby Stuart, Mathijs De Vaan, Paul Ginsparg, Yian Yin  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** arXiv  
[Paper / arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07723)  
**Relevance:** Audits a very large corpus of scientific references to investigate the prevalence and consequences of non-existent citations.

### 6. HalluCitation Matters: Revealing the Impact of Hallucinated References with 300 Hallucinated Papers in ACL Conferences

### 7. Citation Constraints and Reference Hallucinations in Large Language Models
**Authors:** Kimberly Davis, Qusay Mahmoud  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Proceedings of the 39th Canadian Conference on Artificial Intelligence, PMLR 318:464–475  
[Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v318/davis26a.html)

### 9. Large Language Models Reflect Human Citation Patterns with a Heightened Citation Bias

## Citation Generation and Verification

### 10. Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations
**Authors:** Tianyu Gao, Howard Yen, Jiatong Yu, Danqi Chen  
**Year:** 2023  
**Venue:** Proceedings of EMNLP 2023  
[Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.398  
**Relevance:** Introduces ALCE, a benchmark for evaluating LLM-generated answers with citations using fluency, correctness, and citation-quality dimensions.

### 11. RefChecker: Reference-based Fine-grained Hallucination Checker and Benchmark for Large Language Models
**Authors:** Xiangkun Hu, Dongyu Ru, Lin Qiu, Qipeng Guo, Tianhang Zhang, Yang Xu, Yun Luo, Pengfei Liu, Yue Zhang, Zheng Zhang  
**Year:** 2024  
**Venue:** arXiv  
[Paper / arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14486)  
**DOI:** 10.48550/arXiv.2405.14486  
**Relevance:** Introduces a fine-grained claim-triplet framework for detecting hallucinations against reference material.

### 12. RefLens: End-to-End Evidence-Grounded Citation Verification with LLM Agents
**Authors:** SeungHoo Lee, JuneHyoung Kwon, Jooweon Choi, JungMin Yun, Seunguk Yu, Yoonji Lee, Jinhee Jang, YoungBin Kim  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 40(48), 41625–41627  
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42361)  
**DOI:** 10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42361  
**Relevance:** Presents an end-to-end system for citation verification using PDF parsing, evidence extraction, and interactive reports.

### 13. Detecting Citation Hallucinations in Large Language Model Outputs
**Authors:** N. Misra, V. Udandarao  
**Year:** 2026  
**Venue:** Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 40(48), 41325–41327  
[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42257)  
**DOI:** 10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42257  
**Relevance:** Presents a benchmark with valid, partially valid, and hallucinated citation categories and a hybrid verification pipeline.

### 14. CiteSure: Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models for Faithful Biomedical Citation Recommendation

[Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocag122)
